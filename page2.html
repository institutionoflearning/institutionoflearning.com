Free Learning Tips & Short Videos

Small daily habits and 30-second AI micro-lessons to transform your English.

1

Speak every day

Talk aloud for a few minutes about your day, plans, interests or something you learned.

2

Learn in context

Instead of memorizing isolated words, learn useful phrases and create your own examples.

3

Listen actively

Listen to short English clips and notice pronunciation, linking, expressions and new vocabulary.

4

Review regularly

Return to older vocabulary and grammar before adding a large amount of new material.

5

Use mistakes

Write down recurring mistakes and practice the corrected form in several new sentences.

6

Set one clear goal

Choose one specific goal for each study session so you can measure what you actually practiced.
